How it works

Three steps from setup to a certificate people can share.

  1. Create a template

    Give the certificate a title and description, and preview it before you save.

  2. Issue it

    Pick a template, enter the recipient's email, and issue the certificate.

  3. Recipients share it

    Members collect their certificates on a profile page they can point others to.
